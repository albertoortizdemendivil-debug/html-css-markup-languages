Why does the ceremony always fall in October?

France Football schedules the gala once every major European league and both the men's and women's Champions League/Nations League calendars have settled, so voters can judge a full season before casting their ballots.

Balón de Oro gala moves to London for its 70th edition

August 5, 2026

France Football and UEFA have confirmed that the 2026 ceremony will be held on 26 October at The London Palladium, the first time the gala has ever left Paris.

The change marks the award's 70th anniversary and pays tribute to Stanley Matthews, the English forward who won the very first Ballon d'Or back in 1956.

Alongside the men's and women's top prizes, the night will also hand out the Kopa Trophy for the best young player and the Yashin Trophy for the world's best goalkeeper.

Alavés secures the best start in its history

September 10, 2026

Deportivo Alavés has secured the best start in its history in La Liga. Indeed, it had never before earned 10 points in the first four matchdays of competition.

Deportivo Alavés has started the season with three wins and a draw. They beat Getafe 3-0, drew with Rayo Vallecano, defeated Villarreal 1-0, and continue building a fortress at Mendizorrotza after the 5-2 win over Osasuna.

In just four matches, Alavés has scored 10 goals and conceded only 3. They have also won all three home games, some by wide margins. What do these 10 points out of a possible 12 mean? It means the Glorioso side is in second place, between Barça and Real Madrid, and that it's the best start in their history in La Liga.

The wider picture after matchday six

With six rounds gone, GD already tells much of the story: Barcelona's staggering +22 sets them apart, while just a couple of goals separate several mid-table sides.



Barcelona lead the way on 18 points from six straight wins, well clear of second-placed Real Madrid on 15.

Real Betis match Real Madrid's points tally but trail on goal difference, while Atlético Madrid and Sevilla complete a tightly packed top five.

Alavés have slipped to sixth after their fast start, though a +5 goal difference still keeps them comfortably clear of the bottom of the table, where Getafe and Elche currently sit.
