Supercopa Kickoff

Laprovittola gets his revenge on Barça as Joventut lift a historic first Supercopa

September 20, 2026

Nicolás Laprovíttola, who left Barça for Joventut Badalona this summer, was sublime in the Supercopa Endesa final, guiding his new side to victory over his former club in Badalona.

The win handed La Penya the very first Supercopa Endesa title in the club's history, in a tournament that, for the first time in 18 years, was played without Real Madrid among the four contenders.

  1. Joventut's first-ever Supercopa Endesa trophy
  2. A symbolic win over Laprovittola's former club
  3. Played in front of a sold-out crowd at Joventut's own Badalona arena
Wikipedia
What exactly is the Supercopa Endesa?

Created in 1984, the Supercopa is Spanish basketball's traditional season-opener, pitting the reigning Liga Endesa and Copa del Rey champions against the top sides from the previous regular season in a short knockout event.

Spanish clubs miss out on the Euroleague's highest-paid list

September 16, 2026

According to the latest Euroleague salary rankings for 2026-27, guard Vasilje Micic tops the list on $5.6 million a year with Hapoel Tel Aviv — and remarkably, only four clubs place a player in the entire top ten.

Those four clubs are:

  1. Panathinaikos
  2. Olympiacos
  3. Hapoel Tel Aviv
  4. Dubai Basketball

Neither Real Madrid nor Barça — Spain's two representatives in the competition — appear among the top earners, despite both regularly challenging for the title, a sign of a very different wage structure back home.

World Cup Heroes

Iyana Martín: the birth of a new basketball star

September 14, 2026

At the Women's Basketball World Cup in Berlin, young guard Iyana Martín was the standout performer as Spain beat Germany 81-58 to claim the bronze medal.

Martín finished the game with 22 points, 9 assists and a 28 efficiency rating, repeatedly setting up teammates and hitting key threes to put the game out of reach.

Guard Raquel Carrera also played a key role, catching fire in the third quarter to give Spain a +10 cushion that Germany never recovered from.

Twelve players write a new page in Spanish basketball history

September 13, 2026

Spain's national team beat hosts Germany 81-58 to take bronze at the 2026 Women's Basketball World Cup, only their fourth medal ever at the tournament.

  • Silver — Turkey 2014
  • Bronze — Czech Republic 2010
  • Bronze — Tenerife 2018
  • Bronze — Germany 2026

The team's only defeat of the tournament came against the United States in the semi-finals, the side that would go on to win the gold medal.
