The two misfortunes that befall Gasteiz

A dental clinic closes unexpectedly, leaving its customers stranded

September 8, 2026

A dental clinic has unexpectedly closed in Vitoria-Gasteiz . Vivanta has shut down its premises on Ortiz de Zárate Street without prior notice to its clients . Some of them had appointments scheduled for Tuesday, September 8th, and were met with the closure.

The only notice posted at the clinic is a sign on the door that reads:

"This location is temporarily suspending its activity. You have the entire Vivantadental network of centers to continue your treatment." - Sources: GasteizHoy

However, the closure affects all Vivanta clinics in the country, so clients have no other centers of the brand to go to for their treatments. "There isn't a single center available anywhere in Spain" explains a client from Vitoria affected by the closure.

The news has caught patients with scheduled appointments by surprise. "I spoke with them yesterday because I had an appointment today, and they changed the time," explains one of the affected clients. According to him, after learning of the closure, he contacted the customer service line, and "they took my information and told me that someone from Vitaldent would get in touch ."

The closing of Xu's Chinese Bazaar, one of the oldest in Vitoria

September 2, 2026

Stores are an integral part of life in a city, a neighborhood, or a street. They are places where several generations have gone to buy a last-minute gift, a toy, school supplies, or anything for the home. The Xu family's general store, one of the oldest in Vitoria-Gasteiz, has been one of them.

However, after 27 years in business, the Xu Bazaar will close for good sometime between September and October.

Hua Lan, the owner, has decided to retire after undergoing back surgery. It wasn't a decision she made lightly. “I'm really sad about it because I have no other choice. I just had back surgery,” she says.

Two major victories for sports

Markel Beloki will race in the Cycling World Championship

Markel Beloki will race in the Cycling World Championship for the first time with the Spanish national team. Alejandro Valverde has included the young cyclist from Vitoria in the squad that will compete in the Montreal road race next Sunday, September 27th.


Beloki will share the squad with Juan Ayuso, Enric Mas, Carlos Verona, Iván Romeo, Raúl García Pierna, Igor Arrieta, and Marcel Camprubí. Spain will rely on Ayuso and Mas as their spearheads in the fight for medals. Iván Romeo and Pablo Castrillo will also take part in the time trial.

Having just turned 21, Beloki will be the youngest rider on the men's national team. The call-up confirms his rapid rise within the international peloton and will allow him to take part in one of the most important races on the calendar.

Alavés secures the best start in its history

September 10, 2026

Deportivo Alavés has secured the best start in its history in La Liga. Indeed, it had never before earned 10 points in the first four matchdays of competition.

Deportivo Alavés has started the season with three wins and a draw. They beat Getafe 3-0, drew with Rayo Vallecano, defeated Villarreal 1-0, and continue building a fortress at Mendizorrotza after the 5-2 win over Osasuna.

In just four matches, Alavés has scored 10 goals and conceded only 3. They have also won all three home games, some by wide margins. What do these 10 points out of a possible 12 mean? It means the Glorioso side is in second place, between Barça and Real Madrid, and that it's the best start in their history in La Liga.
