Our story

Horizon Movies opened its doors in 2008 with a single screen and a simple idea: to bring the magic of the big screen back to the neighbourhood. Over the years the cinema has grown into four screens with surround sound and comfortable reclining seats, including Screen 1, equipped with an IMAX 70mm projector.

Today we show new releases, family films and a weekly selection of classics. Our team chooses every film with care, because we believe that going to the cinema should be an experience you look forward to, not just a way to spend an evening.

Horizon Movies is a fictional cinema created by a team of three students as part of the web design module. You can meet them on the team page.

What we believe in

  • Cinema for everyone. Fair prices, a weekly discount day and an accessible building.
  • Quality first. Excellent picture and sound in every auditorium.
  • Listening to our audience. Your comments help us choose better films and improve the cinema.
  • Part of the neighbourhood. We organise school visits and film clubs throughout the year.
