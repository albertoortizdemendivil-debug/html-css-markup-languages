About our cinema

Horizon Movies is a neighbourhood cinema with four screens and surround sound. Screen 1 offers IMAX 70mm projection and the other three screens have digital projection. We show the latest releases, family films and a selection of classic movies every week, so that there is always something for everyone.

On this website you can find out which films are on, how much a ticket costs, what other viewers think about our cinema and who we are. Everything is organised in five simple pages that you can read in just a few minutes before coming to the cinema.

Whether you are planning a night out with friends, an afternoon with the family or a quiet evening on your own, you can use the menu at the top of every page to move around. The highlighted option always shows the page where you are.

Why choose Horizon Movies

  • Comfortable seats. Wide reclining seats with plenty of legroom in all four screens.
  • Great picture and sound. IMAX 70mm projection in Screen 1, digital projection in the other three and surround sound in all of them.
  • Something for everyone. New releases, family films and classics every week.
  • Fair prices. Reduced tickets for children and seniors, and a discount day every week.
  • Accessible for all. Step-free entrance and reserved spaces for wheelchair users.

Plan your visit

Going to the cinema with us is very simple. Just follow these steps:

  1. Look at the catalog and choose a film and a showtime.
  2. Check the prices to see which ticket suits you best.
  3. Read the reviews if you want to know what other viewers think of us.
  4. Come to our box office, pick your seats and enjoy the film.

Quick answers

When is the cheapest day to go?

Every Wednesday is Cinema Day, with reduced prices on standard tickets. See the details on the prices page.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

We have a snack bar with popcorn, drinks and sweets, so we ask you not to bring outside food into the auditoriums.

How can I send a comment or a suggestion?

Use the contact form on the About us page. We read every message.

Does the website work on my mobile phone?

Yes. The design is responsive, so the pages adapt to the size of your screen.
